POLICY
======

Selection is author-first and editorially piloted.
Only explicit Dutch editions (dut/nld) are eligible.
Existing NL KEEP and REVIEW works are excluded from acquisition.
Study guides, summaries, workbooks and teaching material are rejected.
Collected works and ambiguous matches are routed to REVIEW.
AUTO_ACCEPT requires strong author/title concordance and a validated physical cover.
Per-author caps prevent catalogue domination by prolific authors.
SELECTED and RESERVE contain unique works and unique physical editions.
The reserve is built during the same run to eliminate recovery/final-gap loops.
No invented ISBN, publisher, date, page count or cover.
No bundle modification.
No database write.
