POLICY
======

Selection is author-first and editorially piloted.
Only explicit French editions (fre/fra) are eligible.
Existing FR KEEP and REVIEW works are excluded from acquisition.
Study guides, summaries and teaching material are rejected.
Collected works and ambiguous matches are routed to REVIEW.
AUTO_ACCEPT requires strong author/title concordance and a validated physical cover.
Per-author caps preserve catalogue diversity.
SELECTED and RESERVE contain unique works and unique physical editions.
Reserve is constructed during the same run.
No invented bibliographic metadata.
Page count remains a later global enrichment task.
No bundle modification.
No database write.
